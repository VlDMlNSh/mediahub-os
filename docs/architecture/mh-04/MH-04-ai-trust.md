# MH-04 AI Trust

AI is T4 Intelligence, not T0 Trusted Core.

AI may observe, reason, propose and request. AI output is untrusted input until validated, authenticated in the applicable service context, authorized and policy-checked.

AI cannot directly mutate State Authority, self-elevate, bypass Consumer Contract, or turn generated text/events into implicit commands.

AI failure must permit deterministic core operation where safe. AI-specific implementation is deferred to MH-10.
