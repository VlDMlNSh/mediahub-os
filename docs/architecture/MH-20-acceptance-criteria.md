# MH-20 Acceptance Criteria

MH-20 may become ACCEPTED / FROZEN only after evidence proves:

- authority boundary and State Authority traceability;
- rule, trigger and condition contracts;
- scheduler and time semantics;
- authorization and command boundary;
- idempotency, retry, rate limiting, debounce and hysteresis;
- conflict hierarchy and manual override;
- safety classes and critical-action gates;
- energy model/control/safety;
- self-healing/remediation boundaries;
- loop detection, circuit breaker and quarantine;
- AI/device/media/KG/plugin/UI/policy integration boundaries;
- security, privacy and observability;
- resource governance and offline-first behavior;
- update/recovery and persistence boundaries;
- failure semantics;
- unit/contract/integration/security/safety/reliability/stress evidence;
- contradiction and unknown registers resolved or explicitly accepted as residual risk;
- dependency and traceability matrix;
- traceability to MH-01…MH-20 and P0-03…P0-07.

Absence of evidence means NOT VERIFIED. This document is a gate, not an implementation approval.
