# MH-22 — Acceptance Criteria

MH-22 may be accepted only when:

1. Current release candidate is uniquely identified.
2. Source, build, artifact and dependency provenance are traceable.
3. Required security/privacy evidence is complete.
4. Qualification covers software, OS, runtime, hardware and integrations.
5. Installation, update, rollback and recovery are exercised and verified.
6. Backup restore is executed and validated; applicable DR is exercised.
7. Capacity/performance and safe-degradation behavior are evidenced.
8. Operational ownership, runbooks, monitoring and escalation are established.
9. Critical authority paths remain consistent with accepted architecture.
10. Production governance explicitly approves the qualified candidate.
11. Evidence register and decision log are complete.
12. No critical blocker or unresolved critical unknown remains.

Acceptance does not imply HA, zero downtime, disaster-proof operation, or any other property not separately evidenced.
