- storage and health;
- NIC and topology;
- firmware/EFI;
- Secure Boot / TPM / hardware-backed cryptography;
- sensors, thermal and fan state;
- power-loss, sleep, wake, reboot and shutdown behavior;
- Linux distribution and kernel;
- filesystem and mount layout;
- systemd configuration;
- container runtime;
- sandbox/MAC controls;
- firewall and network topology;
- update mechanism;
- recovery mechanism;
- storage durability;
- production qualification status.

No UNKNOWN may be silently treated as VERIFIED.
