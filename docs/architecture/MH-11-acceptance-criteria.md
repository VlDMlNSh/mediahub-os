# MH-11 Acceptance Criteria

Status: PROPOSED — NOT ACCEPTED / NOT FROZEN

Acceptance requires evidence for:

1. observability boundary;
2. logs, metrics, traces and event separation;
3. audit;
4. health/readiness;
5. diagnostics;
6. observer isolation;
7. backpressure and priority;
8. redaction, privacy and classification;
9. retention;
10. forensic evidence/mode;
11. incident and alerting;
12. correlation and trusted time semantics;
13. diagnostic snapshots;
14. state-vs-observation and desired/observed/actual distinctions;
15. configuration/policy/security diagnostics;
16. AI/plugin/device/media/network/storage/update/recovery/performance boundaries;
17. self-healing and automation interactions;
18. cloud/local-first operation;
19. export and debug controls;
20. development/production separation;
21. tests: unit, contract, security, reliability, privacy and forensic;
22. failure domains and storage boundary;
23. technology evaluation;
24. evidence, contradiction and unknown registers;
25. traceability to MH-1…MH-11 and P0-03…P0-07.

No acceptance follows from architecture text alone.
