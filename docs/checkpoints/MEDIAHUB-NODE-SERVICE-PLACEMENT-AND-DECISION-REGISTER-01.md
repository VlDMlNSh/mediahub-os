## 6. Placement rules and fail-closed gates

1. Do not assign a service to a physical node unless the exact node identity, OS/runtime support, capacity, security, network, data classification and recovery requirements are evidenced.
2. The accepted target topology is a design target, not a deployment manifest. No host capability is inferred from a model name, year or RAM amount alone.
3. Alamo names the local endpoint role in the target architecture; it does not select Ollama, ONNX Runtime, llama.cpp or any other runtime by itself.
4. Claude and Ollama are distinct model/service families. A router may use distinct adapters, but exact routing shape remains proposed until the owner decides and supported interfaces are verified.
5. No silent local/cloud/provider fallback. A route change is a new destination/data-egress decision and must be policy-authorized, visible and auditable.
6. Jarvis and ASTRA remain in the engineering plane. Neither becomes MediaHub Product State Authority; no component, provider, workflow engine, queue, persistence store or telemetry system may create a second authority.
7. Developer mesh, HIGH_SPEED interconnect, user/media/device networks, contributor compute, provider egress and CPRG registration are separate scopes. Reachability, localhost and VPN membership are not authorization.
8. Pool B remains untrusted by default. Pool A is operator-owned but still needs capability, identity, isolation and admission evidence.
9. MH-15 acceptance/freeze and implementation authorization are absent. MH-16 remains proposed/not frozen/not authorized. This artifact does not open those gates.
10. Component Manifest 2.0 selection does not prove installation, implementation, verification, qualification or production admission.
11. Do not start Contour B before Contour A has been deployed under explicit authorization and independently qualified/accepted.
12. No tests, qualification, model/provider calls, credentials, runtime/host inspection, installation, infrastructure changes, deployment, migration, merge, rebase or cherry-pick occurred in this pass.
