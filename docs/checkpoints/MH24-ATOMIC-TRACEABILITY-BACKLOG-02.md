## 4. Closure rule

Each item remains open until its record contains:
1. Exact B1 anchor and SHA.
2. Exact B2 mapping/anchor and SHA, or explicit `not represented / partial / conflict`.
3. Owner decision and approval provenance.
4. Canonical contract/acceptance artifact with exact ref and blob SHA.
5. Implementation commit or explicit `NOT IMPLEMENTED`.
6. Test/run identifiers and immutable artifacts or explicit `NOT RUN`.
7. Independent qualification result or explicit `NOT QUALIFIED`.
8. Dependencies, security/privacy implications, rollback constraints and unresolved risks.

A decision on semantic disposition does not close implementation, verification, qualification or production authorization.
